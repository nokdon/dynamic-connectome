# Source provenance

The project owner clarified on 2026-09-16 that the early JAX implementation was their own code, transferred from another local repository into Dynamic Connectome.

[import_manifest.json](import_manifest.json) records the local DC files and numerical artifacts used to prepare this release, their SHA-256 fingerprints, destination paths, and adaptations. It documents the concrete inputs to the curated package.

See [contributions](../docs/contributions.md) for research ownership and coding-agent assistance, and [dependency notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for separately installed third-party packages.
